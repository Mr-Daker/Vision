Inter
  inter-latin.woff2, inter-latin-ext.woff2
  SIL Open Font License 1.1. https://rsms.me/inter/
  Variable files as served by Google Fonts; only the Latin subsets are kept,
  because the interface renders Latin and Devanagari, and Devanagari comes
  from the system stack (Noto Sans Devanagari) rather than from Inter.
